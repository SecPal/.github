# Slogans

Slogans are short brand lines, not sentences. They do not end with a period.

## Approved Slogan

| Brand  | Exact slogan            |
| ------ | ----------------------- |
| SecPal | `A guard's best friend` |

No slogan is approved for SecPal Assure or SecPal Visit. Do not invent or infer
one from their names, intended direction, or a historical product identity.

## Brand-Plus-Slogan Lockup

The approved brand-plus-slogan lockup is:

- `SecPal – A guard's best friend`

The canonical public footer line 1 prefixes the lockup with `Powered by`:

- `Powered by SecPal – A guard's best friend`

See `footer-wording.md` for the full two-line footer pattern, including the
line-2 `AGPL v3+ | <Source Code label>` rule.

## Separator Rule

Footer and slogan separators use **EN DASH `–` (U+2013) with one space on each
side**.

Correct:

- `Powered by SecPal – A guard's best friend`
- `SecPal – A guard's best friend`

Incorrect:

- `Powered by SecPal - A guard's best friend` (hyphen-minus, U+002D)
- `Powered by SecPal — A guard's best friend` (em dash, U+2014)
- `Powered by SecPal –A guard's best friend` (missing space before slogan)
- `Powered by SecPal– A guard's best friend` (missing space after brand)

## Usage Rules

- Treat the slogan and lockup as official SecPal presentation standards, not as
  additional conditions on AGPL-licensed material.
- Use the exact capitalization and punctuation shown above.
- Do not add a period, exclamation mark, or trailing full stop.
- Do not translate the slogan or the `Powered by` prefix. The English wording is
  the only approved wording for every locale and surface.
- Do not rewrite, paraphrase, abbreviate, or invent local variants for a
  product, repository, region, campaign, or season.
- Do not combine the slogan with technical license identifiers or package
  metadata.

## Rationale

One exact period-free English slogan preserves recognizable SecPal presentation
across locales. The en dash with single spaces provides a consistent,
language-neutral lockup separator.
