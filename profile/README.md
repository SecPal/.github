# SecPal

_SecPal – A guard's best friend_

SecPal is being built as integrated operations software for private security
services operating in Germany. Its direction connects administrative and
operational workflows to safely reduce repetitive work, duplicate data entry
and transfer, disconnected workflows, and unnecessary complexity for users.
This is the intended product direction, not a claim of feature completeness.

SecPal remains pre-1.0 and under active development. Its architecture, domain
modelling, and implementation may change materially during this period; this
status does not imply production readiness.

Open Source is fundamental to SecPal. The public project contains the
independently usable product and the portable technical contracts needed for
self-hosting. SecPal Managed customer and fleet knowledge, commercial policy,
and customer-specific orchestration may remain separate under
[ADR-023](https://github.com/SecPal/.github/blob/main/docs/adr/20260824-public-self-hosting-private-managed-operations-adr023.md).
